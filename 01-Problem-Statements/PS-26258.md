## 📋 Official Problem Statement

### Background

Conventional vacuum desludging vehicles cannot operate in hilly terrains, narrow lanes, or areas where suction depth requirements exceed equipment capacity. No appropriate mechanized desludging solution exists for these challenging environments.

### Problem Description

Develop low cost innovative mechanized desludging models specifically suited to varied climatic zones and hilly terrains. Address challenges such as: extending reach of equipment beyond current limits, overcoming vacuum suction limitations at depth, and managing viscous sludge that requires excess water or manual stirring.

### Expected Solution & Deliverables

A portable, adaptable low-cost desludging equipment system for varied climate zones & hilly terrains in the specialized features including: (a) reach via flexible hose + booster pump systems for deeper pits, (b) extendable handling of viscous sludge with in-situ dilution or mechanical agitation, (c) easy portability & operable on two-wheelers or handcarts in hilly and narrow-lane settings. Should include safety protocols for operators.Potential Impact: Safe FSM in geographically challenging areas; extended reach of mechanized desludging services to all rural households.
