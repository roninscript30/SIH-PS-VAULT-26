## 📋 Official Problem Statement

### Problem Description

Description (Background, Detailed Description and Expected Solution) Background The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of Education, Government of India, undertakes approval-related regulatory activities for technical institutions in accordance with applicable regulations, approval process handbooks and prescribed administrative procedures. Depending on the applicable requirements, institutions may be required to submit or maintain security deposits or Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs) as part of the approval and compliance process. The management of security deposits and FDRs involves maintaining institution-wise records, verifying submitted financial instruments, monitoring validity and maturity dates, tracking renewal or replacement requirements, recording release or refund requests and ensuring that the relevant documents remain available for regulatory verification. When these activities are managed through fragmented records, manual correspondence or multiple tracking mechanisms, it may become difficult to maintain an accurate and up-to-date view of deposit status, pending actions and time-sensitive requirements. There is a need for a centralized and streamlined digital portal that enables systematic management of security deposits and FDRs, reduces repetitive administrative work, improves record accuracy and provides timely alerts to the concerned officials and institutions. Such a system should support transparent tracking throughout the lifecycle of each deposit while ensuring adherence to applicable AICTE requirements and authorized financial procedures. Detailed Description The proposed solution envisages the development of a secure, centralized Security Deposit/FDR Management Portal for the AICTE Regulation Bureau and other authorized stakeholders. The portal should maintain institution-wise and instrument-wise records of security deposits and FDRs, including relevant reference numbers, issuing bank details, deposit amount, issue date, maturity date, validity status, supporting documents and current administrative status, as applicable. The system should facilitate the digital submission and storage of FDR documents and other prescribed supporting records. It should enable authorized officials to review the submitted information, verify available instrument details through legitimate bank-issued verification mechanisms wherever supported, identify incomplete records and flag discrepancies requiring further scrutiny. The system should distinguish between information submitted by an institution and information independently confirmed by an authorized bank or financial source. An automated monitoring module should track maturity dates, validity periods, renewal or replacement requirements and other prescribed timelines. Configurable reminders should be generated before relevant deadlines, while overdue or unresolved cases should be flagged for appropriate follow-up. The portal should also support the recording and tracking of requests relating to release, discharge or refund of security deposits/FDRs, subject to applicable regulations, fulfillment of prescribed conditions and authorization by the competent authority. A centralized dashboard should provide an overview of active, expiring, matured, renewed, released and pending instruments. Authorized users should be able to search records, review document histories, update permitted fields, record scrutiny observations and monitor the status of each case. Automated reports should help officials identify pending verifications, upcoming maturity dates, missing documents and cases requiring intervention. The system should incorporate role-based access, secure document storage, appropriate encryption, audit trails and controlled access to financial and institutional information. Where technically feasible and formally authorized, integration with existing AICTE systems and bank verification services should be considered to reduce duplicate data entry and improve the reliability of records. Expected Solution The expected solution is a user-friendly, secure and integrated Security Deposit/FDR Management Portal that streamlines the complete administrative lifecycle of security deposits and FDRs associated with applicable AICTE approval-related processes. The proposed system should provide the following capabilities:
- Centralized Security Deposit/FDR Repository: Maintain institution-wise and instrument-wise records, supporting documents and status in a single portal.
- Digital Document Management: Enable secure uploading, storage, retrieval and review of FDRs, deposit confirmations and other prescribed records.
- Verification and Discrepancy Detection: Facilitate verification through authorized sources wherever available and flag missing information, inconsistencies or documents requiring further scrutiny.
- Automated Validity and Maturity Tracking: Monitor relevant dates and generate advance reminders for expiring or maturing instruments and prescribed renewal or replacement actions.
- Renewal and Replacement Monitoring: Track submission of renewed or replacement instruments and maintain a complete history of changes.
- Release and Refund Workflow: Facilitate submission, processing and status tracking of release or refund requests in accordance with applicable requirements and authorized procedures.
- Automated Alerts and Escalation: Notify relevant stakeholders about pending verification, approaching deadlines, overdue actions and unresolved cases.
- Dashboard and Management Reports: Provide consolidated information on active, expiring, matured, renewed, released and pending deposits/FDRs.
- Audit Trail and Access Control: Maintain traceable records of submissions, verification results, changes, approvals and actions taken by authorized officials. 10. System Integration and Data Security: Support authorized integration with existing systems and protect confidential financial and institutional information. The prototype should demonstrate measurable improvements in record accuracy, verification turnaround time, timely renewal monitoring, reduction in manual tracking effort and visibility into pending cases. Testing should cover scenarios such as new FDR submission, validity tracking, approaching maturity, renewal, discrepancy identification and authorized release processing. The ultimate objective is to establish an efficient, transparent and accountable digital mechanism for managing security deposits and FDRs, minimizing avoidable administrative delays and enabling the AICTE Regulation Bureau to monitor relevant financial instruments systematically while ensuring compliance with applicable regulations and financial procedures.
