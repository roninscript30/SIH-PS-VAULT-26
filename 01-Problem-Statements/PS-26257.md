## 📋 Official Problem Statement

### Background

Villages across India face grey water management challenges including: (a) land scarcity for treatment systems, (b) annual flooding that renders soak pits non-functional and (c) soak pit overflow due to siltation or discharge rates exceeding absorption capacity. Existing GWM solutions are not fully adapted to these conditions.

### Problem Description

Develop innovative, low-cost greywater management models requiring minimum land and portable plug & play mechanized solutions for household and community level. Explore greywater management technologies flood prone villages. Also address the challenge of soak pit overflow when discharge rate exceeds absorption capacity.

### Expected Solution & Deliverables

A modular, minimum-footprint greywater treatment system (vertical biofilter, raised baffled reactor or modified constructed wetland) for household and community level use; a flood-resilient GWM design for flood-prone villages; and a low-cost soak pit overflow management solution (pre-filter, expanded capacity design, or secondary leach field) and bypass-enabled greywater drainage network to ensure continuous wastewater management during flooding or Upgradation of existing greywater drains with flood-resilient bypass provisions to ensure uninterrupted drainage during inundation.Potential Impact: Elimination of stagnant greywater; reduced mosquito breeding; groundwater protection; scalable solution for diverse agro-climatic zones. Enhanced resilience and sustainability of rural sanitation infrastructure through uninterrupted wastewater conveyance and treatment.

> This problem statement requires a physical hardware prototype submission.
