## 📋 Official Problem Statement

### Background

Solid Waste Management has been implemented across all rural villages in India. The primary challenge lies in establishing an end-to-end, self-sustaining dry waste management system tailored for the rural ecosystem.

### Problem Description

Development of low cost solution for ready to use Solid Waste management technologies for villages on 4 stream source segregation, aggregation and last mile collection and processing.

### Expected Solution & Deliverables

Simple, low cost, low-bandwidth software or lot tools that operate effectively in low-connectivity rural zones. Demand-based or dynamic route optimization for collection vehicles with low-cost digital tracking at village-level aggregation points and establishing forward linkages for revenue generation from the waste.Potential Impact: Establishing end-end value chain for overall Solid Waste Management through low-cost technology and converting mixed/MLP plastics into construction materials (e.g., paver blocks, fencing posts, roof sheets) or durable goods.

> This problem statement requires a physical hardware prototype submission.
